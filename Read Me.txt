Hello hello, and welcome to hdtv's Yoshi's Island resource pack for SMB1R (that's me!).

Currently this pack is very very messy and unfinished, so be please be award that a lot is subject to change.

This pack is not accepting contributions at this moment, thank you for understanding.

Some specific things I want to bring to your attention:

	- The audio balancing on sound effects sucks right now, that will be fixed in the future.
	- Yoshi replaces Mario for the time being. In the future Mario will be Mario, and Yoshi will be a bonus custom character.
	- A few things don't work correctly yet because of SMB1R bugs/limitations. I *hope* those will be fixed, but I can't promise it.
	  Some of these bugs are:
		- Sidesteppers face the wrong way during gameplay.
		- On/Off Switches On state animation is messed up due to "loop_offset" not working on it yet.
